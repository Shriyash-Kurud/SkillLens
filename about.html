About SkillLens

SkillLens is an AI-based resume analysis system designed to help students and job seekers understand the strengths and weaknesses of their resumes.
The goal is to provide users with a simple way to evaluate their resumes and receive practical suggestions for improvement.

Why SkillLens?

Clear

Easy to understand resume feedback.

Practical

Suggestions focused on real improvements.

Accessible

Simple resume analysis without complicated tools.
